About Lambasingi

Lambasingi (also Lammasingi) is a serene village in the Eastern Ghats of Andhra Pradesh, famous for being the only place in South India where temperatures occasionally drop below freezing, earning it the nickname "Kashmir of Andhra Pradesh." Surrounded by coffee, pepper, and apple plantations, it offers misty valleys and a unique cool climate.

❄️ Unique Frost Phenomenon

During December–February, mercury can dip to 0°C, creating frost on grass and car roofs. The area recorded light snowfall in 1998, 2006, and 2018 – a rare event in southern India.

The village lies about 100 km from Visakhapatnam, at an altitude of 1,000 metres. It's inhabited by the Bagata tribal community, known for traditional farming and weaving. The drive through winding roads and dense forests is an attraction in itself.

Top Attractions in Lambasingi

Camping Sites

Near Thajangi Reservoir

Multiple camps with bonfire, tents, and starlit nights. Experience sub-10°C nights with modern amenities and guided experiences.

Sunset View Point

2 km from village

Highest point offering breathtaking sunset over misty Eastern Ghats. Perfect for photography and witnessing the golden hour over valleys.

Apple Orchards

Kothapalle Region

Rare apple cultivation in South India; visit during July–September to see the fruiting season. These orchards are a testament to the unique climate.

Seasonal Waterfalls

Streams around Lambasingi

Small waterfalls during monsoon and early winter, perfect for photography and short treks through the forested landscape.

Coffee Estates

Gudem Kotha Veedhi

Organic coffee plantations with guided tours explaining the process from bean to cup, including fresh brew tasting sessions.

Trekking Trails

Around Thajangi

Scenic trails through shola forests and hilltops, moderate difficulty. Best explored with local guides who know the terrain well.

Mist Photography

Entire Valley

Heavy mist blankets the valley from November to February, creating ethereal landscapes ideal for photography enthusiasts.

Tribal Village Visit

Lambasingi Village

Interact with Bagata tribe, learn about their traditional crafts, bamboo work, and sustainable lifestyle in the Eastern Ghats.

Lambasingi – Climate & Tribal Heritage

1998

First Recorded Snowfall

Light snow surprised locals and made Lambasingi famous as the coldest spot in South India, attracting tourists and researchers to this unique microclimate.

2006

Second Snowfall Event

Another rare snowfall confirmed Lambasingi's unique weather pattern, with temperatures dropping to -1°C and frost covering the landscape.

2018

Recent Frost & Snow

Temperature dropped to -1°C, with light frost and ice reported. This event reinforced Lambasingi's reputation as South India's coldest destination.

Bagata Tribe

Indigenous Community Heritage

The Bagata tribe has inhabited these hills for centuries, practicing shifting cultivation and producing exquisite bamboo crafts. Their sustainable lifestyle offers valuable lessons in harmony with nature.
